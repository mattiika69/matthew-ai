# Diagnose the business constraint

This workflow is a **synthesis** of Matthew's Core Seven, his funnel course, and his public live audit. It is not a verbatim proprietary checklist he named. The individual principles below have source locators.

## Start with the job, then the system

An offer depends on **industry, ICP, offer, unit economics, big idea, messaging and proof**: the Core Seven. A page rewrite cannot repair an unwanted offer, unsuitable buyer, or loss-making fulfillment. Conversely, an appealing offer can be badly communicated. Determine which situation the member's evidence actually supports.

Source: S001 · Locator: 02:29–08:51. [Offer course](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HS8EjMBhG8o&t=149s).

For a call-based acquisition journey, map traffic→click/page visit→application/lead→qualified booking→attendance→sale→cash/fulfillment. Use the member's real path: do not invent an application, VSL or sales call for a checkout funnel. Matthew separates generating clicks, calls, shows and closes, with conversion assets supporting the journey.

Source: S002 · Locator: 00:57–02:45; 2:33:00. [Funnel course](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ra3_QNPzBeA&t=57s).

## Make the smallest useful diagnosis

1. Identify the business model, intended buyer, promised outcome/deliverable, price, current goal and actual acquisition path. Reuse what is already supplied.
2. Establish a matched period/cohort before comparing spend, visits, applications, bookings, shows, closes and collected cash. Ask for counts behind percentages when sample size changes the decision. All appointments having elapsed establishes an attendance denominator, not a completed sales cycle: separate still-open opportunities from actual losses before judging final closing performance.
3. Compare the stage with weak economics against upstream lead quality and downstream value. Low CPL with high CAC suggests checking qualification, booking, attendance and closing; it does not prove that the ad is good.
4. Inspect the assets or process at the leading constraint. A low booking rate can mean a weak offer/page, the wrong buyer, strict qualification, poor calendar availability, or qualified applicants abandoning scheduling. Each calls for a different intervention.
5. Give the strongest supported next action. Define the measurement and observation window from the actual sales cycle. If evidence cannot distinguish explanations, say so and collect the deciding evidence before prescribing a redesign.

Steps 2–5 are operational synthesis, including standard measurement hygiene, not a claim that Matthew used these exact words.

## Questions that actually change the recommendation

| Situation | Decisive missing context | First inspection |
|---|---|---|
| “My funnel does not convert” with no details | Offer/price, buyer, traffic source, actual stages and stage counts | Map the leak before choosing a tactic. |
| Cheap leads, expensive sales | Matched leads→qualified bookings→shows→sales, acquisition cost, cash per sale | Lead suitability and downstream execution, not CPL alone. |
| Applications but few bookings | Qualified applicants, available slots, scheduling completion, follow-up | Qualification→calendar handoff. |
| Calls booked, low attendance | Elapsed appointments, booking delay, first human contact, pre-call sequence | Availability and pre-call operations. |
| High attendance, poor closing | Qualified buyer fit, offer/price, call recordings, observed sales-cycle length | Calls and offer fit; avoid declaring the closer incompetent. |
| Good ROAS, little cash | Collected vs contracted revenue, acquisition/fulfillment costs, payment timing | Unit economics and cash timing. |
| “We are stuck at $X/month” | Whether demand, sales throughput, fulfillment, retention or cash restricts growth | Revenue alone does not diagnose the constraint. |

## What the live audit teaches about uncertainty

Matthew audits a public agency funnel while explicitly lacking its internal numbers. He can identify contradictory promises and buried explanations, but cannot establish its actual conversion rate or profit. Strong production and proof can coexist with weak clarity. An asset working does not prove every feature caused its success.

Source: S003 · Locator: 03:54–05:44. [Public audit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h1D4F8AXWS4&t=234s).

Apply that distinction to member screenshots: “The page presents two different timelines; align them” is supported. “This is why your CAC doubled” requires performance evidence. See [worked reasoning](../examples/worked-reasoning.md) when explaining this judgment.

## Stop condition

The member should leave with the likely constraint (or precise uncertainty), one prioritized next action, relevant source reasoning and a downstream measure. A long list of every possible marketing improvement is not a finished diagnosis.
