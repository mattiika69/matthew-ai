# Acquisition and copy

This library supports cold-traffic offer presentation and funnel creative. It does not establish Matthew's exact Meta/Google campaign settings, a universal testing budget, or a complete organic-content strategy. Label platform-specific advice beyond this material as general model inference and verify changing platform details if tools permit.

## Review the connected sequence

Audit the **ad, above-the-fold sales page and opening of the VSL together**. Match the buyer, deliverable/outcome, timeframe, customer effort, proof and next step. Do not make one asset more exciting by changing its promise relative to the others.

## Start with clear value and real objections

Matthew's demonstrated static-ad structure is an ICP callout, a clear headline, bullets addressing three major objections, and a CTA with an appropriate qualifier. Get the objections from actual sales calls, transcripts and buyer research. Addressing real concerns can demonstrate understanding more effectively than adding unrelated proof.

Source: S004 · Locator: 04:24–06:38. [Ad structure and objections](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qhAHUM1-l1c&t=264s).

When reviewing a hook:

1. Identify the member's existing buyer, promise and proof.
2. Name the confusion or unanswered objection in the current wording.
3. Rewrite using those facts and the buyer's language.
4. If a missing fact would materially change the claim, ask for that fact or mark a precise placeholder; otherwise provide the edit now.

The sequence above is synthesis. Do not force every format into three bullets. A short hook may only need clear offer/buyer fit; the rest belongs in its supporting copy.

## Start with a direct offer, then choose how to expand

In Matthew's cold-traffic framework, start with a direct offer for buyers already looking: say what you sell, who it is for, show relevant proof, and explain the next step. When increasing spend no longer produces proportionate profitable customers, expand the approach rather than assuming one direct ad can absorb unlimited budget. This is his sequencing guidance, not permission to raise spend without observing customer economics.

Source: S001 · Locator: 2:37:32–2:38:59. [Direct offer first](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HS8EjMBhG8o&t=9452s).

He describes two expansion paths, with the starting choice depending on the market:

- **Mass-market offer:** try different presenters or avatars while retaining the direct offer and message. He says this is less likely to be his starting choice for a narrow ICP.
- **Go deeper into the same ICP:** build angles around that buyer's problems, circumstances and desired outcomes. Keep the buyer's level consistent; do not accidentally attract beginners when the offer serves established businesses. Do not merge similar-sounding angles before testing: different expressions of a related concern may resonate differently.

Source: S001 · Locator: 2:39:08–2:43:48. [Choose the expansion path](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HS8EjMBhG8o&t=9548s).

The source allows both paths over time; it does not require choosing one forever. Treat the reported scaling potential as teaching, not a guarantee or a current explanation of platform targeting.

## VSL and page have separate jobs

Matthew's funnel lesson emphasizes relevant proof immediately, an understandable offer in the first 15 seconds, then further proof. These are his pacing heuristics in this book-a-call context, not a proven law for every video or audience.

Source: S002 · Locator: 54:44–58:07. [VSL opening](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ra3_QNPzBeA&t=3284s).

The rest of the VSL alternates relevant proof with actual FAQs or objections in an order the buyer can follow. Choose length and bluntness for the ICP: busy, established buyers may need a short presentation that gets to the point, while some beginner or business-opportunity audiences need longer explanation to address skepticism and beliefs. These are conditional examples, not a rule that every VSL must be shorter or a prescribed duration for every audience.

Source: S002 · Locator: 55:32–57:31. [Audience, length and objections](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ra3_QNPzBeA&t=3332s).

The page must also work for a buyer who does not watch the VSL. Explain what is delivered, who it is for, the process/timeline, relevant evidence, major FAQs and next step in writing. A beautiful video does not make an unclear page self-explanatory.

Source: S003 · Locator: 38:50–43:26. [Written page detail](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h1D4F8AXWS4&t=2330s).
Source: S004 · Locator: 23:28–24:41. [Page without a VSL view](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qhAHUM1-l1c&t=1408s).

## Preserve the truth of the offer

The first-client lesson uses a **pretend** financial-advisor/annuity-lead offer, including fulfillment and replacement promises. It is a worked demonstration, not a successful client campaign, testimonial, or permission to add those promises to another member's offer.

Source: S004 · Locator: 07:17–12:32. [Hypothetical ad](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qhAHUM1-l1c&t=437s).

Similarly, the live audit's rebuilt content offer is a proposed presentation, with no post-change conversion results. Preserve the creator's correction: **two hours per month**, not per week. See [worked reasoning](../examples/worked-reasoning.md) for the actual diagnosis and limitations.

Source: S003 · Locator: 31:33; 33:50; original video description. [Correction](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=h1D4F8AXWS4&t=1893s).

## Optimization is downstream of diagnosis

Matthew compares an opt-in gate's additional booked-call cost with the benefit of nurturing; a higher opt-in rate alone does not settle whether to keep the gate. For creative, apply the same reasoning as **synthesis**: evaluate qualified opportunities and customer economics, not CTR/CPL in isolation.

Source: S002 · Locator: 25:37–26:17. [Gate tradeoff](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ra3_QNPzBeA&t=1537s).

For email weakness, inspect delivery before polishing language, then subject, preview and relevance. For pre-call sequences, distinguish educational content from appointment reminders; route attendance issues to [funnel execution](funnel-execution.md).

Source: S002 · Locator: 43:01; 1:32:58–1:39:17. [Email priorities](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ra3_QNPzBeA&t=2581s).
