## Conflicts and corrections

### The offer course focuses on front-end acquisition

Source: S001 · Locator: 09:05–09:31. Matthew explicitly limits this course to the front-end intro cold-traffic offer, excluding upsells and recurring services. Keep this scope when applying its 2× upfront-cash objective; extension to another revenue stream requires separate reasoning and must not be attributed as an established universal Matthew rule.

### Close-rate targets have different contexts

Source: S001 · Locator: 2:05:50. The September 14 offer lesson uses a 20–25% close-rate check in its cash-flow diagnosis.

Source: S002 · Locator: 2:00:31–2:01:43. The September 25 funnel lesson discusses a 15–30% live-call close-rate target and distinguishes booked-call forecasting from attended-call performance.

Use the later explicit denominator for a live-call diagnosis; retain the earlier target when discussing that checklist. Do not merge into a supposedly universal range or infer a complete reversal of opinion.

### Margin is subordinate to the cash objective

Source: S001 · Locator: 2:05:26; 2:13:01. The 80% margin diagnostic is conditional; a subsequent 70% example is accepted because it meets the larger 2× cash target. The exception is explicit within the same lesson.

### Correct the demo's effort typo

Source: S003 · Locator: 31:33; 33:50; original YouTube description. Correct wording is **two hours per month**, not per week. The description is an explicit creator correction and takes precedence over an erroneous demo line.

### Do not reverse conditional probabilities

Source: S001 · Locator: 1:33:49–1:34:10. The transcript reports 95% of third-purchase customers had previously bought leads and then treats this as implying a 5% chance of activation for first-time buyers.

Source: S002 · Locator: 1:04:08–1:04:28. A later passage uses 91%/9% and mentions prior six-month form data.

**Curator mathematical correction:** The share of experienced buyers among retained customers is not the retention rate of inexperienced buyers. Both group base rates are needed. Preserve the qualitative lesson about experience and retention, not the unsupported 5%/9% probabilities. The underlying records were not obtained; the 95%/91% difference could reflect cohorts or dates and remains unresolved.

### Examples are not measured wins

Source: S003 · Locator: 03:54–05:44; 1:12:06–1:13:14. Public live audit with proposed changes; no internal metrics or after-test outcome.

Source: S004 · Locator: 07:17–12:32. Explicitly hypothetical lead-agency offer; invented demonstration promises, including speed at 11:09 and replacements at 11:25.

Source: S001 · Locator: 14:58–28:20; 2:05:06–2:13:09. Illustrative economics models, not customer case studies.
